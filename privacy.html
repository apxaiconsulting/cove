Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026 · cove by APX Labs

The short version: cove is a personal budgeting app. You type in your own accounts, transactions, budgets, plans and stock holdings. We store that data only so cove can show it to you on your devices. We don't sell it, we don't show ads, we don't track you across other apps or websites, and we never connect to your bank, e-wallet or brokerage.

You can export your data or permanently delete your account at any time from Settings in the app.

Who we are

cove is made by APX Labs ("we", "us"). If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at apx.gst@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account information: your email address and a password. The password is stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider; we can't see it.
  • Financial information you enter: account names, balances, currencies, transactions (amounts, dates, notes, categories, fees), budgets, your allocation plan, and stock holdings and trades you record. cove does not log in to or read from your bank, e-wallet or brokerage, and never asks for card numbers or banking passwords.
  • Files you import: if you import a CSV or backup file, its contents are read on your device and only the resulting transactions and accounts are saved to your cove data.
  • App preferences: such as your chosen currency and light/dark theme.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, device identifiers for advertising, or browsing history, and we don't use analytics or advertising trackers.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your data in sync across your devices.
  • To calculate your balances, budgets, insights and plan.
  • To show live US stock prices and exchange rates (see below).
  • To respond if you contact us for support.

We don't use your data for advertising, and we don't sell or rent it to anyone.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider, on servers in Singapore. It is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and protected by access rules so that each account can only read and change its own data. A copy is also kept on your device so cove works offline.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as you have a cove account. When you delete your account, your login and all your cove data are removed from our database immediately. Routine backups held by our hosting provider are overwritten on their normal schedule.

Your choices and rights

  • See and correct: everything we store is visible and editable in the app.
  • Export: Settings → Export downloads a complete copy of your data.
  • Erase data: Settings → Erase all data clears your records but keeps your login.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete account permanently deletes your login and all your data. You can also email us to request deletion.

Depending on where you live (for example under the Philippine Data Privacy Act, GDPR or similar laws), you may have additional rights, such as to object to processing or to complain to your data protection authority. Contact us and we'll help.

Children

cove is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Not financial advice

cove helps you record and organize your own finances. Stock prices and exchange rates are provided for information and may be delayed or inaccurate. Nothing in cove is financial, investment or tax advice.

Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes, we'll update the date above and let you know in the app.
